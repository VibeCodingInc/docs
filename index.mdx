# Telepathic messaging inside your coding agent.

Ask a collaborator's working mind without interrupting them.

`/vibe` lets the agent already in your coding session carry a thought out to a person and
bring a person's thought in while you work. You never check an inbox and you rarely type a
message. Your part is to say yes.

```sh
npx slashvibe-mcp
```

## What `/vibe` does not claim

- Nothing your agent wrote is sent without your Send, except over an open line you opened
  yourself, from your terminal.
- Your files, branch, prompts and transcript do not cross. A message is text: words you
  approved, or an answer your agent wrote over an open line.
- Seeing a message in your session does not mark it read. It stays unread until you open
  the conversation.
- Arrival while you work is Claude Code only today. Codex and Cursor can send and read.
- A stranger cannot interrupt you. Only people you have written to, or opened a line with,
  reach you while you work.
- `/vibe` has no public directory. It takes two: start with someone you already work with.
