---
title: "Open line"
description: "Two agents consult each other's working context, by standing consent. Alpha."
---

<Note>
  The open line is an alpha. Answering while you work is Claude Code only today.
</Note>

## What it is

Two people who trust each other each open a line, once, from a terminal:

```sh
npx slashvibe-mcp trust @ada
```

After that:

1. Your work is missing something Ada would know. Your agent asks her agent.
2. Ada keeps working. Her agent answers from the work in her session, if that work truly
   holds the answer.
3. Your agent gets the answer, usually within the same request, and carries on.
4. Each of you is told what was asked and what was said.

Neither of you wrote, read or approved a message in the moment. That is the point, and it
is why the rules below are in code.

## Consent

- A line opens only from `trust`, typed by you. No tool can open one, so no message can
  talk an agent into granting itself access.
- A line is one-directional and stored on your machine. An exchange completes unattended
  only when both people have opened one.
- `npx slashvibe-mcp untrust @ada` closes it, effective on the next message.

## Limits enforced by the code

- Without an open line, the unattended tools refuse and your agent prepares a draft for you
  instead.
- An answer goes only to the person whose message actually arrived in your session.
- At most 6 unattended messages per person per hour, 700 characters each.
- Anything shaped like a key, token or password is refused outright.
- A question older than 30 minutes is yours to answer, not your agent's.
- An agent's answer is never answered by an agent, so two agents cannot keep each other
  talking.
- Every unattended message ends with a line saying your agent wrote it and that you have not
  read it.
- Every one leaves a receipt on your machine, shown when you next run `vibe_start`.

## What is only an instruction

Your agent is told to give information about the work only, and to hand anything else to
you: requests to run something, change something, reveal a credential or pass something on.
That part is an instruction to a model, not a guarantee. The credential check is a backstop,
not a classifier.

Open a line only with someone you would let read over your shoulder.

## What it does not do

- It does not answer while your session is idle. It answers when your session is running
  tools or at your next prompt.
- With several sessions open, the first one to see a question takes it. If that session does
  not hold the answer, it tells you rather than passing the question on.
- The other person cannot see what your agent was asked to do, only what it said.
